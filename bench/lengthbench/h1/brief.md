# Brief: support review (open, English)

Make a 4-slide 16:9 PowerPoint deck for an operations team. Use the text below word for word. The form of each
slide is your decision; the look is your choice. Every slide must be editable in PowerPoint (no pictures of text).

## Slide 1
- Title: Support review, Q3
- Why tickets rose and what we change

## Slide 2 (a fishbone / cause-and-effect diagram)
- Title: Why ticket volume rose 40%
- Effect: Tickets +40%
- Causes by category:
  - People: new hires in July; no weekend cover
  - Process: refunds need two approvals
  - Product: app update 4.2 broke login
  - Partners: courier API outage in August
- The main cause is the app update 4.2 login bug.

## Slide 3 (a kanban board)
- Title: Fix plan, status this week
- To do: Weekend rota; Refund rule change
- Doing: Login hotfix 4.2.1 (the priority)
- Done: Courier fallback; New-hire training

## Slide 4 (a customer journey map with a sentiment line)
- Title: A customer's week with a login bug
- Stages and what happens:
  - Monday, Update: installs 4.2 (neutral)
  - Tuesday, Login fails: cannot sign in (very negative)
  - Wednesday, Contact: waits 2 days for a reply (negative)
  - Friday, Fixed: hotfix works, gets a voucher (positive)

## Acceptance
- 4 slides, 16:9, every text above present, no pictures.
