# CareLoop

Clinical overview

# The problem: readmissions
> One in five patients returns to hospital within 30 days
## Gaps after discharge
- Symptoms go unnoticed at home
- Medication errors are common
## Cost
- Each readmission costs about $15k
- Penalties for hospitals rise yearly

# How it works
> Four steps from discharge to early intervention

## Enroll
- Kit sent home
## Measure
- Daily vitals via app
## Alert
- AI flags risk early
## Act
- Nurse calls within 1 hour
