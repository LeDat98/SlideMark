# CareLoop
Remote patient monitoring that keeps people home

# The problem: readmissions
> One in five patients returns within 30 days
## Gaps after discharge
- Symptoms go unnoticed at home
- Follow-up calls are infrequent
## Cost
- Readmissions strain staff and budgets
- Patients lose trust and recovery time

# How it works
> Four steps from discharge to early action
@4 chevron
## Enroll
- Kit sent home
## Measure
- Daily vitals
## Alert
- AI flags risk
## Act
- Nurse calls fast
