# Compound interest
> Interest on interest makes 1,000 USD grow to about 1,629 USD in 10 years
@1:1
## Formula
```math
A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}
```
- P: principal, r: annual rate, n: periods per year, t: years
