# SWOT: Online Grocery Startup
@2x2
## Strengths
- Fast delivery
- Fresh local sourcing
- Strong app UX
## Weaknesses
- Thin margins
- Small brand awareness
- Limited warehouse capacity
## Opportunities
- Growing online grocery demand
- Subscription plans
- New city expansion
## Threats
- Large incumbents
- Rising delivery costs
- Price wars
> Double down on speed and freshness while controlling costs
