# Latency vs. throughput
> Faster engines are not always the highest throughput


## Takeaway
- Lower latency often means lower throughput
- B offers the best balance
- C maximizes throughput at 25 ms
