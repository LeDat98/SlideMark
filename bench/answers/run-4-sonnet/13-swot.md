# SWOT: Online Grocery Startup
@2x2
## Strengths
- Fast delivery
- Fresh local sourcing
- Easy app
## Weaknesses
- Small scale
- High delivery cost
- Low brand awareness
## Opportunities
- Growing online demand
- Subscription models
- Partnerships with farms
## Threats
- Big retailer competition
- Thin margins
- Supply disruptions
> Leverage freshness and speed to win local niches
