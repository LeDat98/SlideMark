# Risks and mitigations
> Hiring speed is the main risk; every wave has a go/no-go gate
{hl="Clinician hiring falls behind" widths=3:1.2:1.9:5:1.2 align=lccll}
| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation | Owner |
|-|-|-|-|-|
| Clinician hiring falls behind | High | Medium | Two recruiting partners, sign-on bonus pool $0.8M | COO |
| Slow app adoption in Valley | Medium | Medium | Phone booking kept, community nurse outreach | CMO |
| Lab hub delay | High | Low | Interim contract with regional lab | CFO |
| Lease costs above plan | Medium | Medium | Cap rent at 9% of revenue, walk-away clause | CFO |
^ Gate criteria per wave: 90% of staff hired, systems signed off, rent within cap
